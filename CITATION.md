# Citation guide

**Research author:** Hayden Austin

The repository contains multiple independent claims rather than one paper or
one model. Cite the narrowest object that supports your use.

## Repository-level citation

> Austin, Hayden. *AI Culture Mind: Claim-Separated Mathematical and
> State-Space Research*. Version 2026.08.25, 2026.
> <https://github.com/xSolumx/AI_Culture_Mind>.

## Scientific citation contract

Include all of the following when they exist:

1. **Research author:** Hayden Austin.
2. **Programme:** the canonical charter under
   [research-programs](research-programs/README.md).
3. **Claim document:** the theorem, preregistration, result report, or model
   contract actually used.
4. **Artifact:** the JSON, checkpoint hash, exact certificate, or artifact
   manifest entry supporting the reported number or theorem.
5. **Version:** repository commit and, for models, model/checkpoint version.

Do not cite a broad README as evidence for a numerical result when the linked
result document and artifact are available. Do not cite a smoke test, partial
log, or preregistration as a completed result.

## Claim language

Preserve the evidence label used by the source:

- exact theorem or computer-assisted theorem;
- exact reduction with an open residual condition;
- bounded numerical evidence;
- empirical result under stated seeds/data/budget/hardware;
- negative or failed gate;
- historical or superseded interpretation;
- open conjecture.

The [repository map](REPOSITORY_MAP.md) identifies the canonical source for
each kind of claim.

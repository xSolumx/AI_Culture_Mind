## Purpose

This Code of Conduct outlines our expectations for participant behavior, how we handle reports, and how community conduct relates to the project's licensing and contribution expectations.

## Quick summary (suitable for README)

Be kind, be respectful, and follow the repository license and attribution requirements. If you experience or observe unacceptable behavior, report it (see Reporting below). Contributors agree to license contributions under AGPL-3.0.

## Expected Behavior

Community members are expected to:

- Be respectful and considerate in communication and actions.
- Assume good faith and focus on constructive collaboration.
- Listen when others raise concerns and take them seriously.
- Give and receive feedback graciously and responsibly.
- Respect personal boundaries and privacy.
- When contributing code or documentation, include or preserve appropriate license headers and attribution where relevant.

Impact matters more than intent: even unintentional harm should be acknowledged and addressed. If someone tells you that your behavior caused harm, listen, apologize if appropriate, and take steps to make it right.

## Unacceptable Behavior

The following behaviors are not acceptable and may lead to removal from project spaces, loss of contribution privileges, or other enforcement actions:

- Harassment, threats, or abusive language.
- Persistent unwanted attention after a clear request to stop (including "sea-lioning").
- Insults, character attacks, or demeaning comments about a person or group.
- Stereotyping, discrimination, or exclusion based on identity or background.
- Sexualized behavior, unwelcome sexual attention, or sexual content in inappropriate contexts.
- Sharing someone's private or confidential information without permission (doxxing).
- Encouraging, threatening, or glorifying violence or other harm.
- Retaliation against someone for reporting a concern or participating in an investigation.
- Repeated bad-faith reporting intended to harass or burden maintainers or volunteers.
- Removing, altering, or claiming other licensing or copyright notices on project materials in ways that violate the repository's AGPL-3.0 license or contributors' attribution.

This list is not exhaustive. Behaviors that undermine the well-being of the community are also covered.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all spaces managed by the Solum Triality project or Solum Solus, including the issue tracker, pull requests, mailing lists, chat channels, events, and any other project-organized forums. It also applies to conduct related to project contributions, including code, documentation, and other shared materials.

## Reporting

If you believe someone is violating this Code of Conduct, or you have concerns about licensing or improper attribution, you can report it in one of these ways:

- Preferred: Use the repository's Code of Conduct issue template: create a new issue and select the "Report: Code of Conduct" template (or use the link provided in the repository). This creates a structured report that helps maintainers respond quickly.
- Email: haydaust@gmail.com

When reporting, include as much relevant information as you can: what happened, when and where it happened (links if applicable), who was involved, and any supporting evidence. If the issue involves license or attribution concerns, include file paths, commits, and any relevant license headers. Reporters may request confidentiality; we will do our best to respect it while fulfilling our responsibility to the community.

## Enforcement

Reports will be handled by the project maintainers and, when appropriate, short-term or ad-hoc conduct stewards. After a report is received we will:

1. Acknowledge receipt of the report as soon as practical.
2. Assess the situation and, if needed, gather more information from the parties involved.
3. Determine appropriate actions, which may include warnings, requests to correct behavior or attribution, temporary or permanent removal from project spaces, revocation of contribution privileges, or other remedies.

Enforcement decisions aim to be fair and proportional to the behavior and its impact. For incidents involving license violations or improper removal of AGPL-3.0 attribution, we may request that contributors restore appropriate license headers or take other corrective steps; in serious cases we may escalate to the hosting organization or pursue other remedies consistent with the AGPL-3.0 license and applicable law.

## License header snippet (copy-paste)

When adding new files, contributors are asked to include a short notice. Example one-line header (replace YEAR and NAME):

> Copyright (c) 2026 Solum — licensed under AGPL-3.0-or-later

A more complete recommended header (multi-line):

"""
Copyright (C) 2026 Solum

This file is part of the Solum Triality project and is licensed under the GNU Affero General Public License v3.0 or later (AGPL-3.0+).
For full license text see: https://www.gnu.org/licenses/agpl-3.0.en.html
"""

Please preserve existing headers when modifying files.

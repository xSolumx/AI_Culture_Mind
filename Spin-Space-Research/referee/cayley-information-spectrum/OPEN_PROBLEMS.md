# Open problems and nonclaims

The package proves no statement beyond the domain in [`THEOREM.md`](THEOREM.md).

## Not proved here

- Global five-query D-, A-, or E-optimality across all allocations.
- Optimality among nonorthogonal or correlated probe frames.
- The unrestricted seven-variable Dirac--Gram inequality.
- Positivity of every orientation margin in the unrestricted reconstruction.
- A complete classification of exceptional mixed five-probe stabilizers.
- Any advantage for a neural sequence model.
- Any computational advantage over direct, delta-rule, fast-weight,
  Householder, quaternion, or complex/MIMO recurrences.

## Most important mathematical question

The canonical-family algebra is complete. The most valuable external check is
the orbit-completeness bridge: does the shared `Spin(8)` action, together with
the exact same-view pair gauges, reduce every orthonormal balanced design to
the displayed one-parameter family with no hidden discrete or continuous flag
invariant?

If that bridge is rejected, the canonical-family theorem remains exact but the
complete-orbit corollary must be withdrawn or narrowed.

## Separate research line

The Dirac--Gram program asks whether a cubic Gram-volume penalty controls
nonorthogonal designs. Its signed-star and one-edge theorems, Fourier-energy
bound, and endpoint-face theorems are independent results. None is needed for
this Cayley-spectrum proof, and none should be bundled into its headline.

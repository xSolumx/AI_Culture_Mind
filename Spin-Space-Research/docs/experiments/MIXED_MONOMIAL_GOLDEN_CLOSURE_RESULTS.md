## Question

Let

\[
N\cong2_+^{1+6}:\operatorname{PSL}(2,7),
\qquad |N|=21{,}504,
\]

be the exact signed-monomial group generated by the seven maintained left
octonion operators and the two maintained signed-Fano generators. Separately,
the fixed binary-icosahedral embedding supplies an $A_5$ vector image and two
faithful $2.A_5$ half-spin images in the same eight-dimensional basis. Is the
group generated by $N$ and any of those golden-field images finite?

The answer is no for all three views. A single word of length three proves it.

## Arithmetic infinite-order proof

Put

\[
c=\frac{1-\sqrt5}{4}.
\]

The exact characteristic polynomials are

\[
\begin{aligned}
p_v(x)=p_-(x)
={}&x^8+\frac{\sqrt5-1}{2}x^7+c x^6+c x^2
+\frac{\sqrt5-1}{2}x+1,
\end{aligned}
\]

and

\[
\begin{aligned}
p_+(x)={}&x^8-x^7+c x^6+\frac{\sqrt5-1}{2}x^5
+\frac{1-\sqrt5}{2}x^4\\
&+\frac{\sqrt5-1}{2}x^3+c x^2-x+1.
\end{aligned}
\]

The integer ring of $\mathbb Q(\sqrt5)$ is

\[
\mathcal O_{\mathbb Q(\sqrt5)}
=\mathbb Z\left[\frac{1+\sqrt5}{2}\right].
\]

The coefficient $c$ is not in this ring. Equivalently, its field trace and
norm are $1/2$ and $-1/4$, so its monic quadratic polynomial over
$\mathbb Q$ does not have integer coefficients.

If $T_\rho$ had finite order, all its eigenvalues would be roots of unity.
Every characteristic coefficient would then be an algebraic integer, because
it is an elementary symmetric polynomial in algebraic integers. The displayed
coefficient $c$ contradicts that implication. Therefore

\[
\boxed{\operatorname{ord}(T_v)
=\operatorname{ord}(T_+)
=\operatorname{ord}(T_-)=\infty.}
\]

The artifact also computes the rational characteristic norm

\[
\operatorname{Norm}_{\mathbb Q(\sqrt5)/\mathbb Q}(p_\rho)
=p_\rho\,p_\rho^\sigma.
\]

Its coefficient denominators are exactly $\{1,2,4\}$. A monic polynomial
whose roots are all roots of unity is a product of cyclotomic polynomials and
lies in $\mathbb Z[x]$. Thus the nonintegral norm supplies a second exact form
of the same non-cyclotomic obstruction.

## Shortest-word audit

The symmetric maintained alphabet contains 17 distinct monomial steps after
adjoining inverses. The vector pair contributes three distinct symmetric
steps, while each half-spin pair contributes four. The replay checks every
mixed word of length two in one cyclic order:

| View | Exact mixed products | Orders observed |
|---|---:|---|

Every one of the 187 products has the listed finite order by exact matrix
powering. Reversing the two factors does not require another screen: $hn$ is
conjugate to $nh$ by $h$. Words whose two letters lie in the same source group
are finite because the two source groups are finite. Consequently
$F_AF_Bb$ is a shortest infinite-order word in this symmetric generator
alphabet.

## What this settles

For the repository's fixed relative basis conventions, this proves:

- the mixed monomial/vector-$A_5$ group is infinite;
- both mixed monomial/$2.A_5$ half-spin groups are infinite;
- the finite order-864,000 common-carrier triality closure does not remain
  finite after adjoining the monomial operator group;
- no finite faithful action on a spanning 240-point $E_8$ root configuration
  can realize any of these exact mixed closures; and
- the failure is visible at word length three even though every mixed word of
  length two remains finite.

This is a particularly useful negative result: extensive cyclotomic behaviour
at the generator-pair layer was real, but it did not extend to the generated
group.

## Claim boundary

The result proves infinitude, not topological or Zariski density in $SO(8)$.
It does not classify every relative embedding of the two abstract finite
groups, identify a new abstract infinite group, or demonstrate an ML-quality
or kernel-speed advantage. Determining the identity component of the compact
topological closure is the next group-theoretic question.

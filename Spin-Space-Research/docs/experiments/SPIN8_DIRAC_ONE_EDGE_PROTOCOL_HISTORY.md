# Variable-Cayley One-Edge Protocol History

The execution transcript records the ordering; no version was independently
timestamped by a separate commit.

## Version 1: prospective falsifier

Written before results. It fixed the family, exact four-character Walsh gate,
200,000-sample and 32-restart adversarial attack, and the rule that a numerical
survivor is not a theorem.

Outcome: the exact Walsh support was `{1,egc,adgc,ade}`. The random maximum log
advantage was negative; optimization converged to the orthonormal equality
manifold with only float64 roundoff above zero.

## Interstitial exact slice audit

Before any full tensor grid, exact one-variable reconstructions measured the
degree and boundary factors of `F`, `P^2`, `Q^2`, `R^2`, and `PQR`. A second
independent slice then divided the signed sectors by the inferred amplitudes
and confirmed the low degrees of `H1`, `H2`, and `H3` in every variable.

## Version 2: prospective full reconstruction

Frozen after the slice audit but before either full grid. It records the sector
amplitudes, exact low multidegrees, two disjoint `4 x 4 x 4 x 4 x 6` grids,
direct map equality, and eight all-sign exact holdouts. Positivity still
requires every tetrahedral principal-minor polynomial; interpolation alone
cannot promote the theorem.

## Execution correction and final certificate

The initial reconstruction artifact retained `holdouts_pending: true`, but an
early result summary mistakenly described the preregistered eight-times-32
holdout gate as complete. On 2026-08-06 the missing gate was implemented and
run without changing its points, signs, or acceptance criterion: all 256 exact
direct determinant comparisons matched.

Repeated crashes in a monolithic determinant expansion also prompted an
implementation-only amendment. The same frozen polynomial was evaluated in
five fresh-process stages: sparse determinant construction, lower Duffy chart,
upper Duffy chart, boundary layers, and assembly. No theorem criterion was
changed. The final assembly passes only when it links the original
reconstruction, the completed holdouts, every lower-order principal-minor
certificate, and both exact chart summaries.

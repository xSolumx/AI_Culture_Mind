Current interpretation: the memory write law is no longer the main empirical
bottleneck. Under one router, direct and triality-coded slots have the same
ordinary overwrite capacity. Coarse-to-fine block routing improves learned
retrieval for both direct and delta memories; hard routing is exact only in
the synthetic linearly separable alias world and degrades at the adversarial
key-noise frontier. The supported Spin(8) advantage is cross-view action and
routing completion. Spin(9) supplies exact 9-to-16 Clifford binding and a
possible Hopf coarse index, but no same-width capacity theorem.

The final matched Task B action row is now empirically closed on prospective
seeds `20`--`29`. Shared actions remain exact at length 2,048, while the
parameter-richer independent family averages `0.542924`; forcing the same
router leaves that result unchanged. Direct and standard delta memories agree
exactly under the common hard routes. The older metric-only replay failed its
strict reproduction gate and remains a separate provenance warning.

The general transported delta recurrence has a stable co-moving compiler into
official FLA chunk/recurrent kernels, with a declared 128- versus 64-scalar
state mismatch. The subsequent frozen large-slot campaign supplies the missing
measured sparse-router layer: 64 overlapping keys, shared-three-view versus
independent controls, physical gathered state, and a fused inference kernel.
It is still not a model-level benchmark, learned action discovery, or evidence
of extra Spin(8) capacity.
The visual atlas keeps those quality, local fused-state, matched-core, official
FLA, and co-moving compiler protocols separate, and identifies the next bridge
as an FLA-compatible selected-block mixer with cache and backward contracts.

The exact compiler lineage now also distinguishes coefficient field from real
representation type. Declaring \(\mathbb Q(\sqrt2)\) allows a genuinely new

triality modules. This algebraic extension and the exact
\(\mathrm{Cl}(3,0)\hookrightarrow\mathrm{Cl}^0(1,4)\) signature chain are
representation results only; they do not alter the memory-capacity verdict.

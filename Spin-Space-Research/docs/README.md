# Documentation Guide

This page is the front door to the research record. It organizes the material
by purpose and evidentiary status without moving or rewriting frozen historical
documents.

For claim-level rather than chronological navigation, begin with the
[single research-programme index](../../research-programs/README.md). For an independently
readable theorem audit, use the
[balanced Cayley-spectrum referee package](../referee/cayley-information-spectrum/README.md).
The [public release boundary](../PUBLICATION_SCOPE.md) explains why some local
proof-search products and private working notes are intentionally absent.

> **Present boundary.** Several constrained theorems and exact reductions are
> established. The unrestricted signed Dirac--Gram inequality, global
> equal-five-query optimality, and a triality-specific sequence-model advantage
> remain open. The memory-core programme has nevertheless completed its
> hierarchical routing and full-transport CUDA gates: triality and direct
> slots tie under the same router, hierarchical routing improves both direct
> and delta retrieval, and a co-moving compiler enables the official FLA
> chunk recurrence. A prospective ten-seed Task B replication also supports
> shared cross-view action completion while preserving exact direct/delta
> parity. A later 64-slot overlapping-semantic cohort passes shared routing and
> hierarchy gates `10/10`, while a fused physical gather beats both eager
> controls on the named RTX 2070 SUPER. These results do not establish extra
> storage capacity or settle model-level quality.

## Evidence labels

| Label | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Exact theorem** | The conclusion follows from stated premises by symbolic argument or exact finite verification. |
| **Exact reduction** | The original question is transformed reversibly into explicit remaining conditions; those conditions may still be open. |
| **Numerical evidence** | A bounded floating-point search found the reported behavior or no counterexample. |
| **Empirical result** | The statement applies to the recorded seeds, data, budget, hardware, and evaluation protocol. |
| **Open** | No complete proof or decisive empirical comparison is claimed. |
| **Historical** | The document preserves what was believed or planned at that date; later records may supersede its interpretation. |

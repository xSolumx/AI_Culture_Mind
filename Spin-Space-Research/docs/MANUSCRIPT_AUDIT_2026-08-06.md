## Executive verdict

The current mathematical story is coherent after correction, but it is not one
single theorem and it is not yet a competitive language-model result. Four
distinct contribution families survive the audit:

1. a sharp generic four-versus-five identifiability theorem for shared
   multiview \(\operatorname{Spin}(8)\) actions;
2. exact balanced Cayley information geometry and a proved variable-Cayley
   one-edge Dirac--Gram family;
3. a general triangular bilinear-scan theorem, of which triality is an
   exceptional equivariant instance;
4. mechanism evidence for shared-family retraction and exact finite-action
   compilation, still awaiting the matched baselines needed for a broader ML
   claim.

The finite second-edge Dirac--Gram problem is reduced exactly but not proved
globally. Global equal-five-query D-optimality, the unrestricted Gram--Cayley
inequality, and a triality-specific language-model advantage all remain open.

## What was audited

The audit had three layers.

### Mechanical layer

Every maintained Markdown file was checked for malformed LaTeX fragments,
unbalanced display and inline math, unclosed code fences, deprecated promotional
language, stale renamed paths, and broken relative Markdown links. The scan
covers the canonical archive, `SSM-Models` excluding its virtual environment,
and both `SpinorModel` generations.

### Semantic layer

The current README, paper draft, plain-language chapter, foundations,
reproducibility guide, research map, literature audit, two-edge status synthesis, and
strategy ledger were read against the latest exact result documents. Dated
result reports were classified by their own evidence level rather than judged
as though every old “next gate” sentence were a current roadmap.

### Integrity and lineage layer

All 305 entries in `ARTIFACTS.sha256` were recomputed from raw bytes. The 463
destinations in `PROVENANCE.json` were compared with their extraction hashes,
and the canonical-to-archive document mirrors were inspected for intentional
versus accidental divergence.

There are 116 same-relative-path Markdown pairs between the canonical `docs/`
tree and `SSM-Models`. After normalizing line endings and terminal whitespace,
104 are byte-equivalent. The remaining 12 differences were inspected: they are
canonical artifact-path relocations, later dated addenda, current formula
typesetting, or the deliberately different historical-status link. No
unexplained mirror drift remains.

The 38 unique external references in the canonical Markdown corpus were also
checked for reachability. Thirty-seven answered the direct HTTP check; the one
timed-out arXiv endpoint (`2606.26560`) was separately resolved and its title
and author metadata verified. Reachability is not a substitute for checking a
paper's claims, so the key triality, design-theory, and SSM statements were
compared with their primary abstracts or papers as described below.

## Corrections made

### Present-day mathematical claims

- The information matrix is now described as a linearized information Gram
  operator, and as Fisher information only under an explicit isotropic Gaussian
  observation model.
- The paper now distinguishes exact computational premises from the logical
  theorem that uses them. Replaying arithmetic is not presented as a substitute
  for proving the inference step.
- The balanced Cayley quotient now separates its classical global orbit theorem
  from the exact repository-specific split-isotropy calculation. An earlier
  quotient display that divided an already reduced flag space by the two pair
  gauges a second time has been corrected.
- The five-probe theorem now states the full allocation-dependent result:
  single-view four-probe designs have a six-dimensional principal stabilizer;
  mixed four-probe designs have a three-dimensional principal stabilizer; and
  every mixed five-probe allocation has an open dense free stratum.
- The Dirac--Gram notation now defines the full-rank domain of
  \(Q=(XX^{\mathsf T})^{-1/2}X\). Singular frames are handled only through the
  equivalent polynomial form and continuous boundary extension; no pseudoinverse
  is implied.
- The second-edge frontier was advanced from the already completed
  boundary-kernel audit to the actual remaining task: endpoint factorization
  and global positivity of four degree-six and four degree-twelve polynomial
  gates.
- The general Intertwiner SchurScan is now described as a stated constructive
  theorem with a finite homogeneous lift and an SO(3) control, not as a theorem
  “waiting to be stated.”

### Historical accuracy

- A reading policy now explains the authority order among current summaries,
  corrections, result reports, artifacts, and frozen preregistrations.
- The extracted source README is explicitly labelled historical. Its
  `SSM-Models/...` commands refer to the source layout, not the standalone
  archive.
- Relocated historical JSON reports now point to their published `artifacts/`
  destinations where current documents cite them.
- The early category-engine sketch is labelled untested and nondeterministic,
  and its previously unclosed code fence is repaired without promoting the
  sketch into the maintained architecture.
- The parent `SpinorModel` README now distinguishes the preserved baseline from
  the additive overhaul. The overhaul reports its one-run throughput measurement
  as a diagnostic rather than a general benchmark conclusion.

### Artifact integrity

One raw artifact, `artifacts/final_summary.json`, had acquired a trailing
newline after extraction. Its semantic JSON content was unchanged, but the raw
hash no longer matched the extraction or live artifact manifests. The exact
initial publication blob was restored. A new streaming manifest verifier and
regression test now check every artifact byte for byte.

## Literature reconciliation

Primary sources support the surrounding classical and modern context without
proving the local claims for us:

- Katz and Shnider prove the Cayley form's unit comass and
  \(\operatorname{Spin}(7)\) stabilizer through triality.
- McRae supplies explicit triality bases and the intersection picture producing
  \(G_2\).
- Cohn, Conway, Elkies, and Kumar show why symmetric vertex checks and design
  status do not settle continuous optimality.
- The current SSM literature makes DeltaProduct, PD-SSM, p-BIM, modern
  delta-rule memories, and Mamba-3 mandatory controls. Diagonal expressivity
  limits motivate noncommutative baselines; they do not prove a rotor or
  triality advantage.

The detailed source-by-source comparison remains in the
[primary-literature audit](LITERATURE_AUDIT_2026-08-06.md).

## What remains before submission

The information-geometry draft is now an accurate synthesis, not yet a
self-contained journal manuscript. Before submission it still needs:

1. formal theorem statements with every domain, regularity assumption, and
   quantifier collected in one place;
2. complete human-readable proofs rather than reliance on linked result files;
3. a conventional bibliography and citation style;
4. a compact certificate table naming each artifact, verifier, hash, and proof
   obligation;
5. independent replay from a clean checkout and, ideally, an external
   mathematical review of the principal-orbit and positivity arguments.

The sequence-model paper is less mature. It requires matched parameter, state,
training-token, and measured-throughput comparisons before any architecture
superiority claim is defensible.

## Historical policy

The archive will not silently rewrite history. It will also not preserve a
superseded interpretation as current truth. The original claim, its evidence,
the later correction, and the present status must all remain findable. That
policy is specified operationally in
[Provenance and History](PROVENANCE_AND_HISTORY.md) and
[How to Read the Experiment Record](experiments/README.md).

The current expository layer follows a hybrid authorship convention. Theorems,
abstracts, and result summaries give grammatical agency to the mathematics,
proof, experiment, or present work. Introductions, methods, and discussions
retain first-person authorship when researchers made a choice, accepted a
limitation, or interpreted evidence. Historical documents retain their
original voice unless a dated correction is appended.

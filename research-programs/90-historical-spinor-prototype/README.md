# Program 90: Historical SpinorModel prototype

## Role

`SpinorModel` preserves the original compact geometric-algebra prototype and a
separate overhaul. It is retained as engineering and research history, not as
the maintained rotor-SSM implementation.

## Boundaries

- Historical checkpoints must be evaluated under their original tensor and
  target contracts.
- Results from the overhaul do not silently replace claims about the original
  model.
- The maintained recurrence contract lives in `SSM-Models`, not here.

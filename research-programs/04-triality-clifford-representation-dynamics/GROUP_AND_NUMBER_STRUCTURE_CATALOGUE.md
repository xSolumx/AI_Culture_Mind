# Group and Number-Structure Catalogue

**Last audited:** 2026-08-17T01:14:10+02:00
**Scope:** claim-bearing groups, loops, and additive finite structures actually
defined, generated, or used as experimental targets in this repository. Casual
literature mentions are excluded unless code or a theorem depends on them.

The catalogue distinguishes four statements that must not be conflated:

- **implemented:** a multiplication/action is present in executable code;
- **classified here:** exact invariants identify its abstract structure;
- **known externally:** the abstract structure occurs in standard literature or
  GAP/ATLAS data;
- **open embedding:** the abstract ingredients are known, but the subgroup
  generated by this repository's particular embeddings is not yet classified.

| \(SO(8)\) | Generic orthogonal control and learned gauge family | A matched control, not equivalent to coupled triality as a representation-labelled model |
| \(Spin(9)\) | Sixteen-dimensional Clifford spin module and Dirac sensing | Exact representation/sensing results; not a maintained sequence model |
| \(Spin(n),\ 3\le n\le12\) | Fixed `2.A5` embedding ladder and spinor branching calculations | Triality is special to \(n=8\); the ladder does not create higher trialities |
| $G_2=\operatorname{Aut}(\mathbb O)$ | Octonion gauge stabilizer and basis-identification ambiguity | Terminal even-length data identifies $G_2\cup -G_2$, not a unique $G_2$ gauge |

The matrix set \(G_2\cup-G_2\) is closed under composition and is abstractly
\(G_2\times C_2\), but the negative coset consists of sign-reversing laws, not
octonion automorphisms. The audit therefore continues to report the two cosets
rather than calling both `G2`.

## Named structures that are not groups

| Structure | Correct category | Why the distinction matters |
|---|---|---|
| Unit octonions | Moufang loop | Raw prefix products cannot be reassociated into a parallel group scan |
| Octonion algebra | alternative nonassociative algebra | Left-operator composition is associative but differs from raw octonion multiplication |
| Clifford algebras `Cl(p,q)` | associative algebras | Their unit groups and Spin subgroups are groups; the whole algebra is not |
| Damped affine recurrence | monoid/semigroup unless transitions are invertible | Scan associativity does not imply a group inverse |
| Collatz affine words | affine semigroup | Kept in Programme 07; not evidence for Spin or triality claims |
| Hamming and binary sensor geometries | vector spaces/matroids/codes | Their additive groups are listed above, but their incidence structure carries more information than the group alone |

## Strongest next novelty test

The best unresolved continuation is now the **identity component of the mixed
monomial/golden closure**:

1. compute the exact commutant and invariant bilinear/tensor spaces of the
   mixed generators to reject reducible, complex, and quaternionic closures;
2. determine all rational relations among the eigenangles of the certified
   infinite-order witness, with an explicit integer-relation bound;
3. conjugate the resulting torus directions by the finite monomial group and
   compute the generated Lie-algebra dimension;
4. prove that the identity component is $SO(8)$ if the Lie algebra is all of
   $\mathfrak{so}(8)$, or identify the proper compact subgroup otherwise; and
5. keep numerical logarithm and rank calculations as discovery only until the
   angle relations and Lie closure have exact certificates.

This is a classification of the closure of a fixed matrix representation, not
a promise of a new abstract infinite group. The established infinitude theorem
already rules out the earlier finite $E_8$-orbit hypothesis for this alignment.

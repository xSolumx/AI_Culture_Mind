# Program 04: Spin(8) sensing and Cayley design

## Object

Mixed observations of one shared infinitesimal `Spin(8)` action through the
vector and two real chiral-spinor representations. This is an identifiability
and exact-design program, not evidence for the `Cl(3,0)` language model.

## Independent claim families

1. **Identifiability:** generic probe counts and stabilizers.
2. **Balanced Cayley spectrum:** exact spectrum and conditioning on the
   orthonormal balanced family.
3. **Exact versus approximate design:** five equal probes and fractional
   probability designs have different feasible sets.

Correlated nonorthogonal Dirac--Gram inequalities now have their own program
because their proof objects, trust boundary, and global obstruction are
different.

## Current boundaries

- Generic five-probe identifiability is not global conditioning optimality.
- The balanced Cayley theorem does not compare every allocation or
  nonorthogonal frame.
- Numerical design searches are falsifiers, not global proofs.

# Supporting evidence and provenance tracks

These are not additional numbered research programmes.

## Controlled model benchmarks

[`Spin8-SSM-Benchmark/`](../Spin8-SSM-Benchmark/) contains matched-path
empirical evaluation against direct, delta-rule, Mamba-family, and other
appropriate baselines. A run is a result only when its structured artifact is
complete and validated. State-, parameter-, and compute-matched comparisons
answer different questions and must stay separately labelled. Out-of-memory
events, partial logs, and smoke tests are operational evidence only.

The next model-level gate is an FLA-compatible selected-block mixer with
recurrent cache, differentiable reference/backward, and fused-inference
contracts, followed by a controlled sequence-model comparison. Existing
memory-core benchmarks are not that model result.

The 2026-08-16 SSM cohort adds transparent Transformers Mamba-2 and
equation-faithful DeltaProduct references to several bounded tasks. The
maintained Pure Spin(8) row wins the frozen supplied-coordinate signed-
transport task in all three seeds, but parameter, recurrent-state, and compute
are not matched and the references are unfused. The final-only octonion cohort
separately distinguishes failed fixed-depth optimization, successful depth
curriculum, and the `G2 union -G2` identifiability symmetry. These results are
canonical under `SSM-Models/experiments/`; they do not replace a natural-task
or production-kernel benchmark.

The 2026-08-17 continuation removes supplied Lie coordinates for a finite
eight-token dictionary. Three fresh seeds identify the local actions and
compose an unseen center-producing relation through L128. A separate v1.1
compiler freezes one passing dictionary and records a 30.7x--67.1x model-
forward speedup with preserved outputs on the local GPU. The identification
result is still synthetic and every-prefix supervised; the systems result is
frozen-table inference and is not a fused-Mamba, training, or continuous-input
comparison.

A later frozen continuation replaces the finite dictionary with unique noisy
continuous observations from a new nonlinear chart per seed. Shared Spin(8)
beats a capable parameter-near/exact-state independent `SO(8)^3` control on all
six L128 splits. A separately frozen RTX 2070 SUPER update allocation matches
the six model-update walls within 2.97% and retains the ordering after giving
generic baselines up to 6,604 updates. The task remains injective,
every-prefix-supervised, synthetic, and backed by Transformers' unfused Mamba-2
fallback; it is not a natural-task or production-kernel benchmark.

The endpoint-only successor keeps that task and candidate set fixed but removes
every intermediate state target. Each L16 sequence supplies only its final
signed 24-real state. Shared Spin(8) records median L128 MSE `0.01296`, versus
`0.06268` for the capable exactly state-matched independent family at equal
updates and `0.09080` under the separately pre-frozen local update-wall
allocation. All 36 checkpoints across both cohorts strictly rehash and reload.
This answers the dense-prefix-supervision objection for the bounded injective
teacher; unsigned/partial observations, chart shift, longer or irregular label
spacing, natural tasks, and fused modern-SSM training remain open.

A further endpoint-observability audit varies only which final triality blocks
enter the loss. Exact rational generator-probe ranks reach 28 from seven basis
states in each view, and a quotient collision proves hidden-lift Bayes MSE
`1/8`. Shared Spin(8) transfers either signed half-spin endpoint across all
three views in every fresh seed. The frozen aggregate is still a failure:
vector-only exact lift selection misses in all three seeds, and one independent
positive-only supervised control remains near chance. All 30 fresh checkpoints
and all regenerated schedules pass integrity; the negative result is not an
execution failure.

The separately frozen calibration successor closes exactly that empirical
failure by supplying a lift-invariant max-coordinate address and one lift-odd
sign alongside `8v`. The complete interface is three address bits plus one
sign bit. Seeds 4--6 pass every gate without median rescue, with shared action
RMSE `0.012825--0.013633`, all-view L128 MSE `0.009198--0.026986`, and exact
lift/center relation rows. The fixed-coordinate sign is worse than vector-only,
locating the successful change in chart conditioning. This does not infer the
address or sign from the vector endpoint alone.

The exact optimizer trace then shows why a longer identical run is not a clean
negative-view rescue for the state-matched independent control: its
negative-specific head has zero data gradient and exactly follows AdamW decay,
whereas every shared Spin(8) coordinate row receives gradient. The stronger
matched falsifier keeps one shared latent head and the same 24-scalar state but
learns independent positive/negative Spin(8) alignments. Its frozen all-view
gate fails on two directly supervised seed-7 vector-L128 cells. Preserving that
failure, the correct alignment wins every action cell, every spinor-L128 cell,
and every fully hidden negative-L128 cell; full supervision confirms that the
scrambled control can fit both alignments. The remaining claim is therefore
bounded cross-view spinor transfer, not universal triality advantage.

## Historical SpinorModel prototype

[`SpinorModel/`](../SpinorModel/) preserves the original compact
geometric-algebra prototype and its separate
[`overhauled/`](../SpinorModel/overhauled/) experiment. It is implementation
lineage, not the maintained model contract. Historical checkpoints retain
their original tensor and target conventions; overhaul results do not replace
claims about the original model.

The maintained rotor recurrence lives in [`SSM-Models/`](../SSM-Models/) and
is governed by Programme 06.

## Result in one paragraph

Raw octonion multiplication has not been made associative. Instead, each unit
octonion is lifted to its real `8 by 8` left-multiplication operator and those
operators are composed associatively. This exactly parallelizes the explicitly
parenthesized recurrence `h_t = u_t h_(t-1)` while retaining an eight-scalar
streaming state. The lift preserves, rather than suppresses, nonassociativity:
the `(e1,e2,e4)` associator has norm 2 and `L(e1)L(e2)` differs from
`L(e1 e2)` by Frobenius norm 4. More strongly, the seven imaginary left
generators and their 21 pairwise commutators have an exact `28 by 28`
coordinate determinant `-2^49`; hence their Lie closure is all `so(8)`, and
products of unit left-multiplication operators generate the connected group
`SO(8)`. The bounded affine extension passes scan, cache, gradient, and
length-4096 stability gates. On the local RTX 2070 SUPER, its ordered
work-efficient operator tree is 3.61 times faster than the Hillis--Steele tree
at length 4096. This is an experimental architecture and eager systems result,
not task superiority or the unrestricted Dirac--Gram proof.

## What was implemented

The module provides:

- the repository's exact oriented Fano multiplication tensor;
- raw, explicitly parenthesized octonion products;
- left and right real multiplication matrices;
- ordered recurrent, Hillis--Steele, and work-efficient operator scans;
- raw eight-scalar streaming recurrence with operator-scan training parity;
- a norm-bounded affine scan;
- an input-selective `OctonionOperatorSSMLayer`; and
- an exact integer Lie-closure coordinate certificate.

The default parallel backend is the ordered Blelloch-style tree already
validated in the sibling SchurScan research. For chronological leaf actions
`A_0,...,A_t`, it returns

`P_t = A_t ... A_0`.

At length 4096 it uses 12,286 matrix compositions instead of Hillis--Steele's
45,057. It does not QR-project intermediate matrices: such a projection would
destroy associative closure and make the result depend on the scan tree.

## Algebra theorem: the lift does not collapse the associator

Let `L_u(x)=u x`. Function composition is associative, so

`(L_u L_v)L_w = L_u(L_v L_w)`

as matrices. But generally

`L_u L_v != L_(uv)`.

Under the fixed Fano convention,

`(e1 e2)e4 = e7`,

while

`e1(e2 e4) = -e7`.

The raw associator is therefore `2 e7`. Acting on `e4`, `L(e1)L(e2)` returns
the right-parenthesized value `-e7`, whereas `L(e1 e2)` returns `e7`. This is
the exact information that a raw octonion prefix reduction would lose.

For unit `u`, norm composition gives

`||L_u x|| = ||u x|| = ||x||`,

so `L_u` is orthogonal. The unit sphere `S7` is connected, `L_1=I`, and the
determinant cannot cross zero; hence every unit `L_u` has determinant `+1`.
The float64 audit over 256 random left and right operators found maximum
orthogonality and determinant residuals `4.44e-16` and `1.78e-15`.

## Exact controllability certificate

Write `T_i=L_(e_i)` for `i=1,...,7`. The available unit-octonion curve

`u_i(theta) = cos(theta) + e_i sin(theta)`

satisfies

`L_(u_i(theta)) = cos(theta) I + sin(theta) T_i = exp(theta T_i)`

because `T_i^2=-I`. Form the 28 skew matrices

`T_i` and `[T_i,T_j]`, for `1 <= i < j <= 7`.

Using their strict upper-triangle entries as coordinates in `so(8)`, the
implementation constructs an integer `28 by 28` matrix with exact determinant

`-562949953421312 = -2^49`.

Therefore these matrices are a basis of `so(8)`. The connected subgroup
generated by the available one-parameter actions has full Lie algebra and is
all of connected `SO(8)`. Thus one token lies on a seven-dimensional
left-multiplication family, but products of tokens are not confined to that
family; first commutators already supply the other 21 infinitesimal directions.

This is an exact certificate and a useful architectural observation. It is not
presented as new octonion mathematics.

## Stabilization theorem

The trainable layer uses

`h_t = d_t L_(u_t) h_(t-1) + (1-d_t) w_t z_t`,

where `||u_t||=1`, `0<=d_t,w_t<=1`, and `||z_t||<=1`. Orthogonality and the
triangle inequality give

`||h_t|| <= d_t ||h_(t-1)|| + (1-d_t)w_t`

and hence, by induction,

`||h_t|| <= max(||h_0||,1)`.

The affine transitions are ordinary maps `(A,b):x -> Ax+b` with associative
composition

`(A2,b2) o (A1,b1) = (A2 A1, A2 b1+b2)`.

Parallel training packs them into `9 by 9` homogeneous matrices. Streaming
applies the raw parenthesized octonion product and retains only eight scalars
per lane. In the frozen float32 length-4096 run:

unit octonion and every target is the complete ordered prefix action

## Haar-basis replication and the `G2` quotient

That robustness gate is now complete. Three deterministic Haar matrices in
`SO(8)` independently transport both input tokens and full prefix-operator
targets. A 28-parameter learned basis begins at identity, crosses its registered
L128 gate in every basis, and reaches MSE `6.77e-8`, `1.54e-9`, and `8.74e-8`.
The fixed-canonical model remains near `0.249`, and the invalid collapsed
octonion remains near `0.216`.

The recovered basis is identifiable only up to the octonion automorphism group.
For `A=Q_hat^T Q`, the audit finds

`A L_u A^T approximately L_(A u)`

with maximum canonical-basis residuals `1.28e-4`, `2.52e-5`, and `2.17e-4`.
The identity residual is at most `9.44e-5`. Thus the learned ambiguity is
numerically a `G2` gauge, as the classical stabilizer theorem predicts.

The frozen replication is **not** an all-gates pass. A 512-parameter dense
linear operator scan fails its `1e-3` AdamW gate in all three bases, and one
float32 oracle is narrowly above its strict `1e-12` threshold. A labelled
post-protocol least-squares estimator uses only legal position-1 training
prefixes and identifies that same dense map at L128 MSE at most `9.38e-12`.
The dense failure is therefore optimization, not capacity. The valid finding
is efficient blind recovery of a transported composition law under a strong
structural prior—not generic superiority over dense operators or SSMs.

## What this changes—and what it does not

Established:

- raw octonionic recurrence can be parallelized exactly by lifting its leaf
  actions to associative linear operators;
- the associator is retained as action information rather than approximated;
- unit left operators generate all `SO(8)` under products;
- the bounded affine recurrence has a sequence-length-independent state bound;
- parallel training and compact streaming implement the same parenthesized
  recurrence; and
- the work-efficient tree materially improves the local eager parallel path.

Not established:

- raw octonion multiplication is associative;
- an accumulated arbitrary `SO(8)` operator fits in eight scalars—the compact
  cache applies only when the recurrent state is the acted-on eight-vector;
- one eight-vector identifies the accumulated operator;
- triality-specific sample efficiency or real-task quality;
- generic superiority over Mamba, DeltaNet, attention, or fused orthogonal scans;
- a production kernel; or
- any unrestricted Dirac--Gram or global D-optimality theorem.

## Research provenance and next falsifier

The classical octonion, norm-composition, and triality background is reviewed
in John Baez, [*The Octonions*](https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0105155), and the
operator/Clifford treatment in Schray and Manogue,
[*Octonionic representations of Clifford algebras and triality*](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-th/9407179).
The ordered work-efficient tree follows the current sibling repository's
[`intertwiner_schurscan.py`](https://github.com/xSolumx/spin-triality-research/blob/main/src/intertwiner_schurscan.py)
implementation and its frozen benchmark result. The reviewed 2026 research did
not supply a credible shortcut that would make raw nonassociative products a
legal prefix operator; the multiplication-operator lift is the clean route.

The next empirical gate should not be another exact table lookup. Train the
new bounded layer on a continuous nonassociative control task whose target
distinguishes `u(vx)` from `(uv)x`, and compare against:

1. an ablation that incorrectly collapses prefixes back to one octonion;
2. a parameter/state-matched dense orthogonal recurrence;
3. DeltaProduct or a comparable delta-rule layer; and
4. Mamba-2 under an explicitly labelled unfused architecture-quality budget.

That benchmark directly tests whether retaining the associator is useful,
rather than merely showing that the algebra is implementable.

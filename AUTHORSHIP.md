## Attribution boundaries

- Third-party papers, libraries, datasets, model weights, quoted material, and
  externally sourced algorithms retain their original authorship and licences.
- Tool-assisted drafting, implementation, or review does not transfer research
  authorship. Git history and artifact metadata preserve the operational
  provenance of individual changes.
- A collaborator or contributor remains the author of their own contributed
  material when explicitly credited.
- A mathematical identity, software dependency, or public benchmark is not
  original repository research merely because it is discussed here.

## How to cite

Use [CITATION.cff](CITATION.cff) for the repository-level citation and
[CITATION.md](CITATION.md) for programme- and result-level guidance. Every
scientific citation should identify the programme charter, the controlling
result or theorem document, and the exact artifact or commit when applicable.
